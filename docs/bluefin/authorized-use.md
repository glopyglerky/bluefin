# Authorized libraries and connected services

Bluefin is an independent media client and server. It does not include a media
catalog, supply copyrighted movies, programmes or music, or grant access to a
subscription service. Bundled preview media is original demonstration content.

Connect only libraries, files, broadcasts, streams and provider accounts that
you own or are authorized to access. Follow applicable law, rights-holder
permissions, your subscription and provider terms. Owning a device, disc or
account does not authorize every form of copying, conversion, recording or sharing.

## Discovery is separate from access

A search result, guide entry, provider link or availability label is information
about a title. It is not proof of entitlement, regional availability, lack of a
blackout, recording permission or a license to play or share that title.
Availability can change. Recheck server or provider authorization for each action.

Some providers require their own app, subscription, supported device or partner
integration. Bluefin opens the official provider app or website when playback
belongs there. A link does not promise playback inside Bluefin. Approved APIs
and licensed data feeds remain subject to their own terms.

## Playback, downloads and sharing

Use downloads, recording, lyrics, karaoke processing, casting and SharePlay only
where your permissions and provider terms allow them. Each participant must
authenticate independently. An invitation, peer address or Iroh connection does
not grant access to another participant's account.

Bluefin must not bypass DRM, payment, subscriptions, geographic restrictions,
authentication, access controls or device limits. Do not use stolen credentials,
unauthorized streams or unlawfully shared media. Bluefin does not obtain
unauthorized media or unlock paid catalogs.

## Separate applications and third-party rights

Connected-library discovery is independent of acquisition tools. The arr
applications are separate projects; Bluefin does not bundle, install or require
them. Optional integrations use supported interfaces and preserve authorization.

Bluefin is not sponsored by or affiliated with Apple, Jellyfin, Plex, Seerr or
connected providers unless expressly stated. Their names identify compatibility
or a user-selected destination. Third-party software, metadata, artwork, lyrics
and trademarks retain their owners' rights and licenses. Bluefin's software
license does not license third-party media.

Applicable open-source licenses and warranty limitations continue to govern the
software. This notice does not establish that a particular activity is lawful,
provide legal advice or replace provider terms. A notice or acknowledgment is
not a substitute for enforcing access controls.
